SuperCube 技术支持

更新日期：2026-07-25

如果你在 SuperCube 中遇到启动、加载、账号、在线匹配或对战同步问题，可以按下面方式处理。

常见处理方式

  • 确认设备已连接网络，在线对战需要访问 match.supercubegame.com。
  • 如果停留在加载页，请退出 App 后重新打开。
  • 如果在线匹配等待较久，可能是当前测试人数不足，可稍后再试或使用另一台设备同时进入在线对战。
  • 如果 Apple 登录失败，请确认设备已登录 Apple ID，并允许使用“通过 Apple 登录”。
  • 如果需要重置本地进度，可在 App 内设置或系统中清除 App 数据后重新进入。

TestFlight 反馈

如果你正在使用 TestFlight 测试版本，请在 TestFlight 中打开 SuperCube，使用“发送 Beta 反馈”提交问题。最好附上设备型号、iOS 版本、网络环境，以及问题发生前的操作步骤。

隐私与账号

SuperCube 支持游客身份和 Apple 登录。隐私政策与账号数据说明见：

https://supercubegame.com/privacy.html

SuperCube Support

Updated: 2026-07-25

If you experience launch, loading, account, matchmaking, or battle synchronization issues in SuperCube, please try the steps below.

Basic Troubleshooting

  • Make sure the device is online. Online battles require access to match.supercubegame.com.
  • If the app stays on the loading screen, quit and reopen the app.
  • If matchmaking takes a long time, there may not be enough testers online. Try again later or enter online battle on a second device.
  • If Sign in with Apple fails, make sure the device is signed into an Apple ID and Sign in with Apple is allowed.
  • To reset local progress, use in-app settings or clear the app data and open SuperCube again.

TestFlight Feedback

If you are using a TestFlight build, open SuperCube in TestFlight and use “Send Beta Feedback”. Please include your device model, iOS version, network type, and the steps that led to the issue.

Privacy and Account

SuperCube supports guest identity and Sign in with Apple. Privacy and account data details are available at:

https://supercubegame.com/privacy.html
